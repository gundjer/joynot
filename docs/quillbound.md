# Quillbound: a walking 3D adventure

The `write-it` route and learning history remain compatible. The earlier illustrated scene with writing underneath has been replaced with a full-screen WebGL world. The original JoyNot logo is unchanged.

## Play loop

Walk a hero across a beveled grid map, uncover fog, collect physical gold tokens, meet the Rune Smith, enchant the Vine Gate, rescue Moonwing, restore the river crossing and meet Pyra. Gates, the river and trees block actual movement. Tap ground tiles for pathfinding, use WASD/arrows, or use the shared touch joystick. Press E/Space or Interact near a glowing encounter ring. Q/Wand spell spends three mana and calms the moss slime temporarily. Health has a brief grace period after contact and a safe checkpoint reset; the equipped rune shield prevents damage. Mana regenerates gradually.

The adventure now narrates its opening and the next objective, with matching captions, a golden path and a floating marker over the destination. Walking close to a character or encounter for 0.6 seconds opens its introduction automatically. Closing it suppresses automatic reopening until the hero leaves the ring; manual Interact still works. The guide introduces the moss slime, explains getting bumped, and explains the river when approaching its bank. Idle hints are limited to two per objective. Where next? repeats the current directions; Listen again replays the latest line. A visible Narration toggle honors the existing saved voice preference and lets the player turn speech on without visiting settings. Missing device speech support leaves captions available.

The wand is labeled **Calm slime · 3 mana**. Its narration describes the cost, six-second duration and distinction from a written bridge spell. The slime is labeled, stops moving and flattens with closed eyes while calmed, and displays its remaining sleep time. Casting again during that time does not spend additional mana. To cross the river, reach the Broken Crossing on the near bank, roll its dice, open the scroll, cast the writing, collect the reward, then follow the golden route over the wooden bridge to Pyra. Dice results, scroll instructions, mistakes and quest outcomes are also read aloud when narration is enabled.

Every encounter opens a temporary scroll within the 3D scene. First roll modeled red consonant / blue vowel dice. Their CVC result powers the word-level rune. Younger players practice the current shape, number or letter instead; word-level players inscribe the rolled word. Higher levels compose sentence spells or a four-sentence letter. Writing uses glow markers and a dry-erase canvas, or the device keyboard for composition. Watch quill demonstrates strokes. Drafts remain after a misfire. The world remains visible around the scroll; no writing panel is permanently placed below the game.

Successful spells animate into the scene. Vines disappear, the bridge becomes walkable, Moonwing follows the hero, and Pyra becomes a small dragon companion after receiving the final spell. Quest chests award gold, mana, collectible stickers/gems, equipment cards and scratch-map cards. Equipment changes the hero model. The character sheet supports equipping saved gear and passing a shared-device family turn. Up to four heroes can choose names, cloak colors and skin tones on the menu. Shared-device attempts belong to the signed-in child's learning record, as explained on the setup screen.

The character sheet also holds a sight-word spell deck: SEE reveals nearby fog, HELP calms the moss slime, and WARM restores a heart, each at a mana cost. After befriending Pyra, a new-expedition button restarts the route while keeping earned gear and gold.

The pocket map is a tactile scratch card: dragging spends one earned card per stroke to reveal a scouting trail. Nearby tiles reveal for free while walking. Current expedition position, opened routes, collected tokens, checkpoint, health and discovered tiles are saved under `extra.quillbound.run`; legacy mana/loot/party remain. Walking autosaves periodically; boss expeditions do not overwrite the normal expedition. Quest rewards are idempotent within an expedition, including after reload. Close the scroll to step away from an encounter without a health penalty.

Writing checks are local, structural checks rather than semantic grading: rune coverage/alignment, sentence card words and length, or four distinct punctuated sentences with linked details and a story reference. Existing answer logs have a 250-character limit. Guided attempts do not count as independent mastery. Boss expeditions use their assigned writing levels and the shared engine's first-try accuracy requirement.

## Deployment

Deploy changed configuration, `app/Games/WriteIt.php`, all new and modified `public/games/write-it` files, `public/games/_joy/js/speech.js` and `public/sw.js`. The shared speech helper now cancels delayed utterances after mute/pause rather than restarting them from its 60ms browser workaround. The 3D renderer reuses existing `public/games/sparkles-rainbow-journey/js/board-scene.js`, its board helper, and `public/games/rustys-after-hours/js/scene3d.js`; include those dependencies if the server does not already have them. No Vite build is needed. Entry assets have a `-quillbound-guided-3` version suffix and the service-worker cache revision is bumped. Run `php artisan optimize:clear` **after uploading**; cache clearing alone does not deploy the game. WebGL is required for this 3D adventure.

## Checks

Run `node --test tests/quillbound-guidance.test.js tests/quillbound-adventure.test.js tests/quillbound.test.js tests/game-assets.test.js` and the PHP suite with `.phpunit-local.xml`. Use `tests/Browser/quillbound-3d.cjs` (or the compatibility entry `quillbound.cjs`) against the isolated SQLite test server described in `tests/Browser/README.md`. It checks actual walking, automatic encounters, route barriers, dice, pointer ink, spell outcomes, bridge traversal, a dragon encounter, scratch cards, loot, persistence and responsive viewport layouts. `tests/Browser/quillbound-guidance.cjs` captures real application speech requests at the browser speech API boundary and verifies greetings, muting/replay, no reopen loop, a stationary calmed slime, bridge directions/outcomes, and responsive guidance. It does not assert audible output quality on physical devices. Previews are saved as `storage/testing/quillbound-3d-*.png` and `quillbound-guided-*.png`.

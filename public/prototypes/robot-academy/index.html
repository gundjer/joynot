HANDS-ON AI

Show it. Test it. Improve it.

1. Tap objects in Collect to add examples. Select both apples and objects that are not apples.

2. Open Train and press Train R0. This prototype learns from simple color and shape features.

3. Open Test and run the delivery. A green apple can confuse a model taught only red apples. Add a green example, retrain, and compare.

Drag the 3D world to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. The camera buttons demonstrate the same world from two roles; real multiplayer comes during game development.

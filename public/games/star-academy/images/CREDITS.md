# Star Academy visual assets

All files are bundled locally. The game does not hotlink external images.

## Surface and star maps

`earth.jpg`, `moon.jpg`, `mars.jpg`, `saturn.jpg`, `sun.jpg`, `saturn-rings.png`, `galaxy.jpg`:
Solar System Scope, https://play.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ .
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
Maps are projected onto illuminated 3D spheres/rings; the galaxy map is displayed as a background. These are mapped visualizations, not live views. No source files were modified.

## NASA imagery

- `blackhole.jpg`: NASA Goddard / Jeremy Schnittman, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/13326/ . Scientific visualization, **not a photograph**.
- `asteroid.jpg`: NASA / Goddard / University of Arizona, https://science.nasa.gov/resource/bennu-mosaic/ . OSIRIS-REx mosaic of Bennu.
- `comet.jpg`: NASA / Bill Dunford, https://science.nasa.gov/resource/comet-c2020-f3-neowise-over-utah/ . Comet NEOWISE photographed from Utah.
- `microgravity.jpg`: NASA, https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/astronauts-bob-hines-jessica-watkins-inside-cupola/ . Bob Hines and Jessica Watkins aboard the ISS.
- `nebula.jpg`: NASA / ESA / CSA / STScI, https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/nebula/en/ . Webb image of the Carina star-forming region.

NASA media guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/ . NASA imagery credits do not imply endorsement. Credit links are visible in destination tours and the in-game image credits panel.

`downloads.json` records the original download URLs. Display crops, circular obstacle tokens, and pan/zoom happen at runtime; originals remain intact.
